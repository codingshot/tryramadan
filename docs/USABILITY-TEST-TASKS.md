# Remote Usability Test Tasks: Ramadan Fasting & Journaling Dashboard

Script for 6–8 tasks that push participants through **profile/location setup**, **fasting (full, break, make-up)**, **meal tracking (fasting and non-fasting days)**, and **journal (write and revisit)**. Use with think-aloud; prompts are for when participants go quiet.

---

## How the app supports each task (for facilitators)

| Task | Where in the app | Notes |
|------|------------------|--------|
| **1 — Profile & location** | Home → “Start your journey” or “I’m Muslim” → Onboarding (Mode → … → Location). Or Dashboard/Settings → Location. | Location step has auto-detect + city search; “Skip for now” goes to Schedule. After setup, Dashboard header shows location; Settings shows “Current location”. |
| **2 — Log full fast** | Dashboard or Dashboard Today → “I’m fasting”. | Timer/countdown and “You’re fasting” confirm. “Mark complete” when day is done. |
| **3 — Break fast mid-day** | Dashboard or Dashboard Today → “Break fast” (visible when fasting) → choose reason. | Day shows as broken; Progress shows “Broken” count. |
| **4 — Make-up day** | Dashboard → open day picker (Today’s plan) → select **yesterday** (or past day) → “Yes, mark complete”. Or Dashboard → Schedule → select day in calendar → “I fasted this day — mark complete”. | Marking a past day complete records it as a made-up fast; Progress “Total days” and list reflect it. |
| **5 — Meals (fasting day)** | Dashboard → Meals, or Dashboard → Schedule → select today → add to Suhoor / Iftar. | Meals page has Suhoor and Iftar tabs; Schedule day view has meal plan + food log. |
| **6 — Meals (non-fasting day)** | Dashboard → Schedule → select a skipped/non-fasting day (or Meals and pick date if available) → add meal. | Meals can be logged for any day; day can be skipped (“I didn’t fast today”) and still have meals. |
| **7 — Journal write** | Dashboard → Journal → pick today → write → Save. | Toast “Entry saved” confirms; empty content shows “Write something before saving…”. |
| **8 — Journal revisit** | Dashboard → Journal → calendar or “View past entries” → click another date. | Calendar shows dots for days with entries; switching date loads that day’s entry. |

---

## Before the session

- **Context:** “You’re trying out an app that helps people track Ramadan-style fasting, meals, and reflections. There are no wrong answers—we care what you notice and how you’d use it in real life.”
- **Think-aloud:** “Say what you’re looking at, what you’re trying to do, and what you’re unsure about. If you go quiet, I might ask what’s on your mind.”

---

## Task 1 — Set up your profile and location

**Scenario:**  
*“Imagine you’ve just downloaded this app because you want to try Ramadan-style fasting. You live in [use their city or a plausible one, e.g. Chicago]. Your first step is to make the app work for where you are so prayer and meal times are right.”*

**Goal:**  
Set a location (or complete onboarding and set/confirm location) so the app shows times for your area. Get to a point where you see something that confirms your location or timezone (e.g. dashboard with local times, or Settings showing the set location).

**Think-aloud prompts (if they go quiet):**
- “What are you looking at right now?”
- “What would you tap or do next to set where you are?”
- “How do you know the app is using your location?”

---

## Task 2 — Log that you’re fasting today (full fast)

**Scenario:**  
*“It’s a fasting day. You’ve finished your pre-dawn meal and stopped eating and drinking. You want the app to know you’re fasting so it can show you the countdown and count this day.”*

**Goal:**  
Start today’s fast in the app (e.g. tap “I’m fasting” or equivalent) and see that the app shows you as fasting (e.g. “You’re fasting,” countdown, or similar). Optionally mark the day as complete when you’re done the task.

**Think-aloud prompts (if they go quiet):**
- “Where did you look first to say you’re fasting?”
- “What does the screen tell you now—do you feel sure you’re ‘in’ a fast?”
- “If a friend asked you how to start a fast in this app, what would you tell them?”

---

## Task 3 — You had to break your fast mid-day

**Scenario:**  
*“Same day. A few hours in, you had to break your fast early—maybe you felt unwell or had to take medicine. You want to record that you broke the fast and why, so it’s not counted as a full fast.”*

**Goal:**  
Record that you broke today’s fast and choose a reason (e.g. illness, medical, other). End with the app showing that today is “broken” (or similar) and not counted as a completed fast.

**Think-aloud prompts (if they go quiet):**
- “Where would you look to say you broke your fast?”
- “How clear is it what will happen if you pick a reason and confirm?”
- “After you did it, how would you check that the app remembered you broke the fast?”

---

## Task 4 — Mark a missed day as “made up” (or record a make-up fast)

**Scenario:**  
*“Yesterday you couldn’t fast at all—maybe you were traveling. Today you’re making up that day: you’re doing a full fast for yesterday. You want the app to reflect that you ‘made up’ that day or that yesterday counts as completed.”*

**Goal:**  
Record yesterday as a completed fasting day (e.g. via calendar/day view or “mark complete” for that date), or use the closest option the app offers for “make-up” or “I fasted that day.” End with yesterday showing as completed (or equivalent) in progress/stats.

**Think-aloud prompts (if they go quiet):**
- “How would you tell the app that you fasted yesterday, not today?”
- “If there’s no ‘make-up’ button, what’s the next best thing you’d try?”
- “Where would you look to see that yesterday is now counted?”

---

## Task 5 — Log what you ate for your pre-dawn meal and your break-fast meal (fasting day)

**Scenario:**  
*“It’s a fasting day and you’ve already started your fast. You want to log what you had for your pre-dawn meal and what you plan to have (or had) when you break fast, so you can look back later.”*

**Goal:**  
Add at least one item for the pre-dawn meal (suhoor) and one for the break-fast meal (iftar) for today. Get to a place where you can see both meals (or both meal types) for today—e.g. Schedule, Meals, or dashboard.

**Think-aloud prompts (if they go quiet):**
- “Where did you go to log food for today?”
- “How did you tell the app which meal was pre-dawn and which was break-fast?”
- “How would you check that both meals are saved for today?”

---

## Task 6 — Log a meal on a day you didn’t fast

**Scenario:**  
*“One day this week you didn’t fast—maybe you were sick or traveling. You still had breakfast and dinner. You want to log those meals so your meal history is complete, even though that day isn’t a fasting day.”*

**Goal:**  
Pick a day that is not a fasting day (e.g. a skipped/day-off or a past day with no fast), go to meal logging, and add at least one meal for that day. Confirm that the meal appears for that date and that the app still shows the day as “not fasted” or skipped if that’s the case.

**Think-aloud prompts (if they go quiet):**
- “How did you choose which day to log?”
- “Did anything in the app suggest you shouldn’t log meals on a non-fasting day?”
- “Where do you see that the meal is tied to that specific day?”

---

## Task 7 — Write a short journal entry for today

**Scenario:**  
*“You’ve had a meaningful day—maybe a tough fast or a good moment with family. You want to write a few sentences in the app’s journal so you can look back during or after Ramadan.”*

**Goal:**  
Open the journal, write a short entry for today (a few sentences), and save it. Be able to see that the entry is saved (e.g. still visible when you leave and come back, or a clear “saved” state).

**Think-aloud prompts (if they go quiet):**
- “What did you look for to find the journal?”
- “When you wrote something, how did you know it was saved?”
- “Would you use this in real life to jot down one thing from the day? Why or why not?”

---

## Task 8 — Revisit journal entries from the last few days

**Scenario:**  
*“It’s been a few days and you’ve written a couple of journal entries. You want to look back at what you wrote on two different days—maybe to compare how you felt or to show a friend.”*

**Goal:**  
Open the journal and view entries for at least two different dates (e.g. today and one or two days ago). Switch between dates and see the correct content for each. If the app has a “past entries” or list view, use it to choose which day to open.

**Think-aloud prompts (if they go quiet):**
- “How did you get to an entry from a previous day?”
- “When you changed the date, was it clear which day’s entry you’re looking at?”
- “If you wanted to find something you wrote last week, how would you do it?”

---

## Optional shorter run (6 tasks)

If you only have time for 6 tasks, use:

1. **Task 1** — Set up profile and location  
2. **Task 2** — Log a full fast  
3. **Task 3** — Break fast mid-day  
4. **Task 5** — Log meals on a fasting day  
5. **Task 7** — Write a journal entry for today  
6. **Task 8** — Revisit journal entries over several days  

Drop **Task 4** (make-up day) and **Task 6** (meals on non-fasting day), or swap one of them in if make-up or non-fasting meal logging is a priority.

---

## After each task (optional)

- “Did you get there? How sure are you?”
- “What was the hardest part of that task?”
- “What would you change so it’s easier next time?”
