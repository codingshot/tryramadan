# Sunni content review

Read the existing `islamic-content-authenticity/SKILL.md` alongside this skill when editing scripture, hadith, Arabic, or religious rules.

- Describe the review as editorial and source-based, never scholar-certified. Do not claim to be an Islamic scholar or make individualized determinations of obligation, sin, validity, fidya, or kaffara.
- Inspect the claim in its rendered context and all duplicated occurrences. Verify exact Quran and hadith references against canonical sources; identify translation or label an educational paraphrase. Do not create quotations or grading from memory.
- Distinguish Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi‘i and Hanbali differences. Do not convert one institution's fatwa into unanimous Sunni consensus. For disputed matters, name the view and source or direct the reader to qualified personal guidance.
- Check dawn versus sunrise, moon-sighting overrides, intention, menstruation/postnatal bleeding, pregnancy/nursing, illness/travel, fidya versus qada, zakat al-fitr versus fitrah, prohibited fasting days, and voluntary practice versus obligatory fasting.
- Do not let gamification judge spiritual merit or penalize legitimate exemptions. Tracking records describe user actions, not legal validity. Shorter practice fasts are not substitutes for obligatory Ramadan fasting.
- Medical treatment is not an app-level religious decision. Avoid blanket injection/inhaler advice; never recommend delaying essential care. Health-benefit claims need identifiable human evidence and must distinguish water-permitted intermittent fasting from Ramadan dry fasting.
- Log reviewed files, source URLs, exact corrections and unresolved questions in `docs/MONOREPO-AUDIT.md`. An audit of selected files is not an audit of the entire corpus. Add regression tests for machine-checkable rules, but do not confuse text assertions with scholarly validation.
