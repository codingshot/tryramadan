# Launch strategy — TryRamadan

**Internal use only.** Detailed launch plan for Product Hunt, launch platforms, and coordinated release. Do not publish.

---

## Overview

**Product:** TryRamadan.app — A free app for the holy month of Ramadan: prayer times, fasting timer, suhoor/iftar plans, cultural education. For Muslims and non-Muslims alike.

**Launch type:** Product Hunt + coordinated social + outreach to journalists, influencers, and communities.

**Target launch window:** Ideally Ramadan Day 1 or within the first week of Ramadan (adjust annually for Gregorian dates).

---

## Launch phases

### Phase 1: Pre-launch (4–6 weeks before Ramadan)

**Goals:**
- Finalize product (feature freeze, QA, accessibility pass)
- Build launch assets (Product Hunt media, press kit, demo video)
- Identify and warm up advocates (friends, beta users, early supporters)
- Soft-prep communities and journalists (no embargo yet)

**Tasks:**
- [ ] Lock feature set; focus on polish and bugs
- [ ] Create Product Hunt tagline, description, gallery (see product-hunt-and-launch-platforms.md)
- [ ] Record 30–60 sec demo video (countdown, prayer times, meal planning)
- [ ] Prepare press kit (screenshots, founder bio, quotes, high-res logo)
- [ ] List advocates who can upvote/comment on PH launch day
- [ ] Identify 10–20 journalists, bloggers, influencers in wellness, Muslim lifestyle, interfaith, tech
- [ ] Draft outreach emails (see outreach-messages.md)

---

### Phase 2: Countdown week (7 days before Ramadan Day 1)

**Goals:**
- Build anticipation
- Drive early installs and onboarding
- Confirm Product Hunt launch date and hunter (if using one)

**Tasks:**
- [ ] Daily or every-other-day countdown posts on social
- [ ] “Get TryRamadan ready” content: set location, reminders, goals
- [ ] Finalize Product Hunt launch date (e.g. Day 1 or Day 2 of Ramadan)
- [ ] If using a hunter: confirm hunter availability and assets
- [ ] Send “heads up” to advocates: “We launch on PH on [date] — would love your support”
- [ ] Pre-schedule social posts for launch day

---

### Phase 3: Launch day (Product Hunt + coordinated social)

**Goals:**
- Maximize Product Hunt visibility (top 5 Product of the Day if possible)
- Drive installs from PH and social
- Create shareable moment (“We’re live on Product Hunt”)

**Tasks:**
- [ ] Publish on Product Hunt at optimal time (12:01 AM PT or early morning)
- [ ] Post launch announcement on Twitter/X, Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook
- [ ] Send outreach emails to journalists (embargo lift)
- [ ] Engage in PH comments: thank supporters, answer questions
- [ ] Pin launch post on social profiles
- [ ] Monitor PH ranking and share progress (“#3 Product of the Day!”)

**Timeline (launch day):**
| Time (PT) | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| 12:01 AM | Product Hunt goes live |
| 6–8 AM | First social posts (Twitter, LinkedIn, Instagram) |
| 8–10 AM | Outreach emails sent |
| Ongoing | Reply to PH comments, retweet shares |

---

### Phase 4: Launch week (Days 1–7 post-launch)

**Goals:**
- Sustain momentum from Product Hunt
- Convert visitors to users
- Gather feedback and testimonials

**Tasks:**
- [ ] Thank everyone who supported on PH (comment, tweet)
- [ ] Share PH ranking and any media coverage
- [ ] Follow up with journalists who didn’t respond
- [ ] Post user testimonials or screenshots (with permission)
- [ ] Light paid boost on social for launch post (optional)
- [ ] Monitor app store / web analytics; fix critical issues fast

---

### Phase 5: Post-launch (2–4 weeks)

**Goals:**
- Capitalize on any coverage
- Submit to additional launch platforms (Indie Hackers, BetaList, etc.)
- Build email list or community for next Ramadan

**Tasks:**
- [ ] Submit to Indie Hackers, BetaList, SaaSHub, AlternativeTo (if applicable)
- [ ] Repurpose PH/coverage for evergreen content
- [ ] Add “As seen on Product Hunt” badge to site if ranking was strong
- [ ] Plan content for mid-Ramadan and Eid

## Risk mitigation

- **Low PH traction:** Have advocates ready to upvote early; engage authentically in comments.
- **Negative feedback:** Respond calmly, thank for input, fix issues if valid.
- **Ramadan timing:** If Ramadan starts on a weekend, PH traffic may differ; consider Monday launch if Day 1 falls on Sat/Sun.
- **Embargo break:** If someone publishes early, adapt; don’t punish — use it as momentum.
