# Content types to post — TryRamadan

Use this as a checklist for what to create and where it fits. Adjust by persona and platform.

---

## Content pillars

1. **Product & features** — What TryRamadan does (fasting timer, prayer times, Quran, recipes, calendar, macros, journal).
2. **Education** — How to fast safely, suhoor vs iftar, Ramadan basics, glossary terms, cultural tidbits.
3. **Culture & food** — Traditions by country, recipes, “Ramadan around the world.”
4. **Community & solidarity** — “We’re here for the whole month,” supporting friends/family, inclusivity.
5. **Wellness** — Hydration, balanced meals, when to break fast, health-first messaging.

## Types of content to post

### Evergreen (use any time)

- **Feature spotlights:** One post per feature (e.g. “Prayer times in your pocket,” “Plan suhoor & iftar in one place”).
- **How-to / tips:** “5 tips for your first Ramadan fast,” “How to break your fast gently.”
- **Glossary / terms:** One term per post (e.g. Suhoor, Iftar, Ramadan Mubarak) with short, shareable definition.
- **Recipe teasers:** One dish per post with photo + link to app/recipes.
- **Culture snapshots:** One country or tradition per post (“How Egypt celebrates,” “Dates around the world”).

### Seasonal (Ramadan and lead-up)

- **Countdown:** “X days until Ramadan — get the app ready.”
- **Day-of launch:** “Ramadan Mubarak. Start your journey with TryRamadan.”
- **Mid-Ramadan:** “Halfway there — how’s your fast going?” + link to progress/dashboard.
- **Last 10 nights:** “Laylat al-Qadr — we’re here for the last stretch.”
- **Eid:** “Eid Mubarak from TryRamadan. See you next Ramadan.”

### Announcements & news

- **New feature:** Short “We added X” with benefit and CTA.
- **New platform:** “TryRamadan is now on iOS / Android / web.”
- **Partnership or collaboration:** If applicable; keep tone aligned with brand.

### Engagement & community

- **Polls / questions:** “Suhoor or iftar — which do you plan first?” “What’s your go-to iftar dish?”
- **User stories / testimonials:** With permission; short quote + optional photo.
- **Quotes:** Hadith or gentle reflections (attribute correctly; avoid altering religious text).

## Format by channel (high level)

| Channel        | Best formats                                      |
|----------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| Instagram      | Carousels (tips, features), Reels (short how-tos), Stories (countdown, polls) |
| Twitter/X     | Short copy + link, threads for how-tos, hashtags   |
| Facebook      | Longer posts, events (e.g. Ramadan start), groups  |
| TikTok        | Short, relatable clips (tips, culture, “day in the life”) |
| LinkedIn      | Product updates, DEI/wellness angles, professional tone |
| Blog/SEO       | How-tos, glossary, culture pieces, “Try Ramadan fasting” type queries |

## Content ideas by persona

- **Non-Muslim curious:** “Try your first fast,” “What is Ramadan?,” “Support your Muslim friends,” glossary terms, culture snapshots, “one app, your pace.”
- **Muslim observer:** “Prayer times + countdown + Quran,” “Your Ramadan in one app,” feature spotlights, last-10-nights, Eid.
- **Wellness:** “Fast safely,” “Health check before you start,” “Macros and hydration,” “When to break fast,” no weight-loss promises.
- **Culture / food:** “Ramadan around the world,” one country per post, recipe teasers, “Plan iftar with recipes from the app.”

---

## Repurposing one piece into many

- **One blog post** → 1 carousel (key points) + 3–5 tweets + 1 LinkedIn post + 1 short Reel/TikTok script.
- **One feature** → 1 Instagram slide, 1 Twitter thread (3 tweets), 1 Story series (3 slides).
- **One testimonial** → Quote graphic for all channels + optional short video if they’re willing.

---

## What to avoid

- Don’t alter or misquote religious text (Quran, hadith).
- Don’t promise health outcomes we can’t support (e.g. “lose weight guaranteed”).
- Don’t stereotype or speak for all Muslims; use “many people,” “for some,” etc.
- Don’t use this folder or internal docs in public copy; keep marketing knowledge base internal.
