## What not to do

- Don’t promise weight loss or specific health outcomes.
- Don’t alter or misquote Quran or hadith.
- Don’t speak for all Muslims (“we all…”). Use “many people,” “for some,” “in our app.”
- Don’t use this file publicly; it’s an internal bank. Copy from it into your actual posts.
