# Outreach messages — TryRamadan

**Internal use only.** Outlines and templates for reaching journalists, influencers, community moderators, and partners. Customize per recipient.

---

## Outreach principles

- **Personalize:** Reference their work, beat, or audience
- **Keep it short:** 3–5 sentences for cold outreach; link to press kit
- **No attachments** in first email (link to press kit instead)
- **Respect embargo** if you set one
- **Follow up once** if no response after 5–7 days

---
