# Pitch and messaging — TryRamadan

**Internal use only.** Use this to keep pitches, taglines, and value props consistent. Do not publish this file.

---

## Elevator pitch (30 seconds)

**TryRamadan is a free app for the holy month of Ramadan.**  
Whether you’re Muslim and want everything in one place — prayer times, fasting countdown, Quran, and meal planning — or you’re not Muslim and want to try fasting and learn about the culture, TryRamadan adapts. You choose your mode, set your location, and get accurate prayer and fasting times, suhoor and iftar ideas from around the world, and a simple way to track your journey. One month, one app, for everyone.

## Objection handling (for sales / partnerships)

- **“Isn’t it only for Muslims?”** — No. We have a Non-Muslim mode: same fasting and culture, tailored content and no religious obligation.
- **“How do you handle prayer times?”** — User sets location once; we show Fajr to Isha (and suhoor/iftar cutoffs) for every day.
- **“Is it free?”** — Yes. TryRamadan is free to use.
- **“What about health?”** — We include a health screening and clear guidance on when not to fast and when to break fast safely.

---

## Voice and tone

- **Clear and helpful** — Explain features and benefits in plain language.
- **Respectful** — Of faith, culture, and individual choice.
- **Inclusive** — “Everyone,” “you choose,” “your journey.”
- **Warm but not casual** — Friendly, not slangy or irreverent about religion.
- **Avoid:** Promises we can’t keep, speaking for all Muslims, altering religious text, weight-loss or medical claims.
