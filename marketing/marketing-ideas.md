# Marketing ideas and campaigns — TryRamadan

**Internal use only.** Brainstorm list: campaigns, growth tactics, partnerships, and creative angles. Pick and adapt by budget and capacity.

---

## Campaign ideas

### 1. “First fast” campaign (pre-Ramadan + day 1)

- **Idea:** Target people trying Ramadan for the first time (Muslim or not). Messaging: “Your first fast doesn’t have to be overwhelming.”
- **Assets:** Short video or carousel: “Set location → Get times → Log your fast.” Testimonial or quote from a first-timer if available.
- **Channels:** Instagram Reels, TikTok, Twitter. Consider a small paid boost for “first Ramadan fast” / “try Ramadan” search or interest.
- **CTA:** Download TryRamadan, complete onboarding before Ramadan starts.

### 2. “Ramadan in one app” (Muslim audience)

- **Idea:** Position as the single app for prayer times, countdown, Quran, and meals. Reduce app fatigue.
- **Assets:** One simple graphic or short clip showing: prayer times + countdown + meal plan (or Quran) on one screen.
- **Channels:** Muslim community pages, Islamic influencers, Ramadan app roundups, app store optimization (ASO).
- **CTA:** “Replace 3 apps with one — TryRamadan.”

### 3. “30 days, 30 traditions” (culture / food)

- **Idea:** One country or tradition per day during Ramadan. Drive daily engagement and showcase culture section.
- **Assets:** Day 1: Egypt; Day 2: Indonesia; etc. — short copy + image or recipe link. Can pull from in-app culture content.
- **Channels:** Instagram carousel or Stories series, Twitter thread, blog or email if you have a list.
- **CTA:** “Explore all 30 in the app” (Culture / Recipes).

### 4. “Suhoor vs Iftar” engagement series

- **Idea:** Light, shareable polls and tips: “Team suhoor or team iftar?” “What’s on your plate tonight?”
- **Assets:** Polls, quote cards, user-generated prompts (“Tag a friend who needs a countdown”).
- **Channels:** Instagram Stories, Twitter, Facebook groups.
- **CTA:** “Plan both in TryRamadan.”

### 5. Workplace / DEI angle (LinkedIn + HR)

- **Idea:** “Support Muslim colleagues (and curious allies) this Ramadan.” Offer employers or ERGs a simple resource: “Share TryRamadan — prayer times, fasting support, no sign-up pressure.”
- **Assets:** One-pager or short LinkedIn post: “How to support colleagues during Ramadan” with link to app. No hard sell.
- **Channels:** LinkedIn, HR/DEI newsletters, partner with one or two ERG leads.
- **CTA:** “TryRamadan — free app to share with your team.”

### 6. Wellness / intermittent fasting crossover

- **Idea:** Reach people already interested in IF who are curious about Ramadan-style fasting (time-restricted, structured).
- **Assets:** “Ramadan fasting is one of the original structured fasts. Here’s how to try it safely.” Tips + app link.
- **Channels:** Wellness creators, health blogs, Pinterest, YouTube “day in my life” collabs.
- **CTA:** “Try Ramadan-style fasting with TryRamadan — health check included.”

### 7. “Last 10 nights” push

- **Idea:** Re-engage users and attract new ones during Laylat al-Qadr. Tone: reflective, supportive.
- **Assets:** “The last 10 nights are here. We’re still here with your prayer times and countdown.”
- **Channels:** All social; consider a gentle reminder in-app or email if available.
- **CTA:** Open the app; share with someone who’s still fasting.

### 8. Eid thank-you and “see you next year”

- **Idea:** Thank the community, share a stat if possible (“X fasts logged this Ramadan”), tease next year.
- **Assets:** Short video or graphic: “Eid Mubarak from TryRamadan. Thank you for fasting with us.”
- **Channels:** All social. Optional: “What should we build next?” survey link.
- **CTA:** Leave a review; follow for next Ramadan.

## Growth tactics (no / low budget)

- **App store optimization (ASO):** Keywords: Ramadan, fasting, prayer times, suhoor, iftar, Quran, Ramadan app. Use in title, subtitle, description. Screenshots that show countdown + prayer times + one more benefit.
- **SEO (web):** Blog or landing pages for “try Ramadan fasting,” “Ramadan fasting app,” “prayer times Ramadan,” “suhoor and iftar times.” Link to app or web app.
- **Hashtag consistency:** Use a small set (e.g. #TryRamadan #Ramadan #RamadanMubarak) so people can find you; add 1–2 per post (e.g. #Suhoor #Iftar) as relevant.
- **Cross-promo:** If you have a newsletter, another product, or a partner, one mention + link during Ramadan can drive a spike.
- **User-generated content (UGC):** Ask users (with permission) for a short quote or screenshot: “How are you using TryRamadan?” Use in stories or testimonials.
- **Reddit / forums:** Participate helpfully in r/islam, r/fasting, Ramadan threads; share the app when it’s a direct answer (“there’s an app that does that — TryRamadan”). No spam.
- **Email (if you collect):** Pre-Ramadan “Get ready” email; day 1 “Ramadan Mubarak”; mid-Ramadan “You’re halfway”; Eid “Thank you.”

## Partnerships and collaborations

- **Mosques / Islamic centers:** Offer TryRamadan as a recommended resource for prayer times and fasting. One sheet or slide they can share with congregation.
- **Universities (MSA / cultural clubs):** “Ramadan resource for students” — prayer times, fasting tracker, safe break-fast info. One partnership or poster can reach many.
- **Wellness or IF creators:** One collab: “I tried Ramadan-style fasting for a day/week with TryRamadan.” Authentic > salesy.
- **DEI / ERG leads:** “Resource for Ramadan at work” — link in Slack or intranet. No cost, high trust.
- **Recipe or culture bloggers:** “Plan your suhoor and iftar with recipes from the app.” They get content; you get backlink and awareness.

## Influencer and creator ideas

- **Micro-influencers (10K–100K):** Muslim lifestyle, Ramadan prep, or “try with me” content. Gift no money if budget is zero; offer early access or a clear value (e.g. “we’ll feature your story”).
- **Format:** “My Ramadan setup” (show app among other tools), “How I know when to break my fast” (countdown feature), “Planning iftar with TryRamadan.”
- **Non-Muslim creators:** “I tried fasting for a day using TryRamadan” — respectful, educational. Great for curiosity-driven audience.
- **Clear rules:** No fake religious authority; no altering hadith/Quran; disclose if paid or gifted.

## Creative angles (copy and hooks)

- **Curiosity:** “What’s it like to fast from dawn to sunset?” “One app that knows suhoor and iftar times for your city.”
- **Relief:** “Stop switching between 5 apps. Prayer times, countdown, and meals in one place.”
- **Inclusion:** “Whether you’re Muslim or just curious — one app adapts to you.”
- **Safety:** “We ask about your health first. Then we help you fast safely.”
- **Culture:** “Ramadan looks different in every country. Explore them all in the app.”
- **Simplicity:** “Set your location once. We handle the rest.”

## Metrics to watch (if you can measure)

- **Awareness:** Reach, impressions, follower growth, share of voice for “Ramadan app” / “TryRamadan.”
- **Acquisition:** App installs (or web sign-ups), traffic from social and search.
- **Activation:** Onboarding completion, first fast logged, first prayer times viewed.
- **Retention:** DAU/MAU during Ramadan; return next Ramadan (year over year).
- **Referral:** “Share TryRamadan” clicks; UGC or word-of-mouth mentions.

## Quick wins (do first if resources are limited)

1. Nail ASO and one strong landing page (or homepage) with clear CTA.
2. Publish a simple content calendar: 1 post every 2–3 days during Ramadan (use social-announcements.md and content-types.md).
3. One partnership: mosque, MSA, or DEI lead — get TryRamadan in front of one real community.
4. Collect 3–5 testimonials or quotes; use in social and app store.
5. One “how to try Ramadan fasting” blog or video for SEO and shares.
