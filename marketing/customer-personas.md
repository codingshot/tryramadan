# Customer personas — TryRamadan

Use these segments to tailor messaging, content, and campaigns. Personas are also reflected in-app (see `src/data/personas.json`).

---

## 1. Non-Muslim curious

**Short name:** Curious explorer  
**Icon:** 🌱

**Who they are:** People who want to experience Ramadan fasting and learn about the culture without religious obligation. Often allies, coworkers, or friends of Muslims; sometimes wellness or spirituality seekers.

**Goals:**
- Learn about Ramadan culture
- Try intermittent fasting in a structured, respectful way
- Support Muslim friends and family
- Spiritual reflection
- Health and wellness
- Build discipline

**Journey (simplified):** Discover → Set up (Non-Muslim mode, health, location, schedule) → Daily use (dashboard, learn, culture, recipes) → Go deeper (guides, glossary, hadith, culture by country).

**Messaging angles:** “Try fasting like a Muslim for the holy month,” “Learn by doing,” “One month, one app,” “For everyone.”

**Where they might find us:** Google (“try Ramadan fasting”), social (Ramadan tips, culture), word of mouth, DEI/wellness content.

**Sample ad angles:** “Your first fast doesn’t have to be overwhelming”; “Try Ramadan with one app — we explain as you go”; “Learn by doing: suhoor, iftar, and culture in one place.”

---

## 2. Muslim observer

**Short name:** Muslim mode  
**Icon:** ☪️

**Who they are:** Muslims who observe Ramadan and want prayer times, fasting tracking, Quran, and spiritual content in one place.

**Goals:**
- Full observance support (prayer, fasting, Quran)
- Spiritual reflection
- Health and wellness
- Build discipline

**Journey:** Discover → Set up (Muslim mode, location for prayer times, schedule, Quran/glossary priorities) → Daily use (countdown, break fast, prayers, Quran, today’s plan) → Observe (prayers page, Quran plan, hadith, culture, emergency break-fast).

**Messaging angles:** “Your Ramadan in one app,” “Prayer times + fasting + Quran,” “Track your fasts, stay on track.”

**Where they might find us:** App stores, Muslim community channels, Islamic content, Ramadan hashtags.

**Sample ad angles:** “Prayer times + countdown + Quran — one app”; “Replace 3 apps with TryRamadan”; “Your Ramadan in one place.”

## 3. Health & wellness focus

**Short name:** Wellness  
**Icon:** 💪

**Who they are:** People interested in intermittent fasting, nutrition, and wellness during Ramadan. May or may not be Muslim.

**Goals:**
- Try intermittent fasting safely
- Health and wellness
- Build discipline

**Journey:** Discover → Set up (health screening, location, schedule, macro tracking if desired) → Daily use (dashboard, Today: hydration, energy, macros) → Track (schedule, food log, macros, health page).

**Messaging angles:** “Fast safely,” “Macro tracking + suhoor & iftar,” “Health-first Ramadan.”

**Where they might find us:** Wellness and IF communities, nutrition content, fitness/health hashtags.

**Sample ad angles:** “Fast safely — health check and structure included”; “Ramadan-style fasting with macros and hydration in mind.”

## 4. Culture & food explorer

**Short name:** Culture & food  
**Icon:** 🍽️

**Who they are:** People who want to explore Ramadan through food and traditions from different countries.

**Goals:**
- Learn about Ramadan culture
- Support Muslim friends and family

**Journey:** Discover → Set up (priorities: culture & recipes) → Daily use (Culture, Meals on dashboard) → Explore (culture by country, recipes by region, add to meal plan).

**Messaging angles:** “Ramadan around the world,” “Recipes and traditions by country,” “Suhoor and iftar from every corner of the globe.”

**Where they might find us:** Food and travel content, culture and DEI channels, recipe searches.

**Sample ad angles:** “Ramadan around the world — recipes and traditions by country”; “Plan iftar with dishes from every region.”

## 5. Quran & learning focus

**Short name:** Quran & learning  
**Icon:** 📖

**Who they are:** Users (typically Muslim) who want to read the Quran during Ramadan and learn Islamic terms and hadith.

**Goals:**
- Learn about Ramadan culture
- Spiritual reflection

**Journey:** Discover → Set up (Muslim mode, Quran/glossary priorities) → Daily use (Quran, Glossary, Learn on dashboard) → Read & learn (juz plan, glossary, hadith).

**Messaging angles:** “Quran + fasting in one app,” “Juz a day, prayer times, and glossary.”

**Where they might find us:** Islamic education, Ramadan apps, Muslim community.

**Sample ad angles:** “Quran + fasting + prayer times in one app”; “Juz a day, with your fasting tracker.”

---

## Cross-persona notes

- **Overlap:** Many users sit between personas (e.g. Muslim + wellness, curious + culture). Messaging can blend benefits.
- **Funnel:** Awareness (what is TryRamadan?) → Consideration (why this app?) → Action (download / start journey). Personas help choose which benefit to lead with.
- **Sensitivity:** Always be respectful of faith and culture. Avoid stereotyping; emphasize choice (Muslim / Non-Muslim mode, priorities).
