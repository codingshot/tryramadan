# Daily Ramadan tips — TryRamadan

**Internal use only.** Use one tip per day (or per post) for social, Stories, email, or in-app. Adapt tone by channel. Do not publish this file publicly.

## Suhoor (pre-dawn)

1. **Eat before the cutoff.** Set a reminder for 20–30 minutes before Fajr so you have time to finish suhoor without rushing.
2. **Protein + complex carbs.** Oats, eggs, dates, and a little healthy fat help you stay fuller longer. Skip the heavy fried foods at suhoor.
3. **Hydrate first.** Drink water before you eat. Your body will thank you by mid-afternoon.
4. **Keep suhoor simple.** You don’t need a feast. A balanced plate and water go a long way.
5. **Know your cutoff time.** Prayer times change every day — check your app so you’re not eating past imsak.
6. **Prep the night before.** If you’re not a morning person, set out suhoor the night before so you can eat and go back to sleep.
7. **Dates at suhoor.** A few dates give quick energy and are a Sunnah. Pair with water and something sustaining.
8. **Avoid too much salt and sugar.** They’ll make you thirstier during the fast.
9. **Fiber keeps you full.** Whole grains, beans, or fruit at suhoor help avoid a mid-morning crash.
10. **Smoothie option.** If you can’t face a full meal, a banana–date–oat smoothie with milk is quick and sustaining.

---

## Iftar (breaking the fast)

11. **Break with dates and water.** Start with 1–3 dates and a glass of water. Give your body a minute before the main meal.
12. **Don’t overeat in one go.** Your stomach shrinks during the day. Eat slowly and stop when you’re comfortable.
13. **Soup first.** Light soup (lentil, vegetable) helps ease your body back into eating.
14. **Save the heavy stuff for later.** Big fried or sugary dishes are easier an hour or two after iftar.
15. **Iftar is a moment.** Use the countdown so you break at the right time — then enjoy the first sip and bite.
16. **Share iftar when you can.** Breaking fast with family or friends is part of the spirit of the month.
17. **Plan iftar ahead.** Know what you’re making or ordering so you’re not scrambling at Maghrib.
18. **Chew slowly.** The first bites after a long fast matter. Slow down and taste the food.
19. **Leftovers are your friend.** Cook a bit extra at iftar and use it for the next suhoor to save time.

---

## Hydration & wellness

20. **Drink between iftar and sleep.** Aim for most of your water in the eating window, not in one go at iftar.
21. **Water > sugary drinks.** Juice and soda are fine in moderation, but water is what your body needs most.
22. **Electrolytes if you’re active.** If you work out or sweat a lot, a little salt and potassium (e.g. banana, coconut water) help.
23. **Listen to your body.** If you feel unwell, break the fast. Health comes first. TryRamadan includes a health check for a reason.
24. **Rest when you need to.** It’s okay to take it easy. The month is long; pacing yourself is wise.
25. **Sleep matters.** Short nights make fasting harder. Protect your sleep where you can.
26. **Sip, don’t chug.** Spread water across the evening instead of drinking a litre at once.
27. **Herbal tea counts.** Caffeine-free tea in the evening can help you relax and add to fluid intake.

## Mindset & intention

28. **Set a small intention each day.** “Today I’ll be patient.” “Today I’ll say one kind thing.” It keeps the month meaningful.
29. **It’s okay to have hard days.** Everyone does. The next day is a new chance.
30. **You’re not alone.** Millions are fasting with you. We’re here in the app when you need a countdown or a reminder.
31. **First fast?** Take it one day at a time. Use the app to see your cutoff and iftar times — one less thing to figure out.
32. **Gratitude helps.** Before iftar, take 10 seconds to be grateful for the meal and the day. It shifts the mind.
33. **Celebrate small wins.** One day completed is a win. Don’t wait until Eid to feel proud.
34. **Comparison steals joy.** Your Ramadan isn’t someone else’s. Focus on your own pace and intention.

---

## Prayer & reflection

35. **Prayer times change every day.** Check your app so you don’t miss Fajr or Maghrib.
36. **Use the last 10 nights.** Even one extra prayer or du’a in the last 10 nights is a gift. We’ll still show your times.
37. **One page of Quran.** If you can’t do a juz, one page or one verse still counts. Consistency over quantity.
38. **Journal if it helps.** A line or two about the day can help you look back and see your growth.
39. **Du’a at iftar.** The moment of breaking the fast is a blessed time for a short prayer or wish.
40. **Fajr is early — set two alarms.** One to wake, one for “last call” before suhoor ends.

---

## Culture & community

41. **Ramadan looks different everywhere.** Explore how different countries break the fast — recipes and traditions in the app.
42. **Support your Muslim friends.** If you’re not fasting, you can still join for iftar or ask how they’re doing. TryRamadan is for allies too.
43. **Try one new recipe.** Suhoor or iftar — pick one dish from a culture you don’t know and try it.
44. **Share the countdown.** Tag a friend who’s fasting: “Almost time to break fast — get the app and never miss the moment.”
45. **Eid prep can wait.** Don’t stress about Eid plans in week one. Enjoy the month first.
46. **Give what you can.** Charity doesn’t have to be money — time, food, or a kind word counts.

---

## Work & daily life

47. **Tell work if it helps.** Many employers offer flexible hours or prayer space during Ramadan. A short “I’m fasting” can avoid misunderstandings.
48. **Avoid the toughest meetings at peak hunger.** If you can, schedule important calls earlier or after iftar.
49. **Keep snacks for non-fasters respectful.** If you’re not fasting, eating at your desk is fine; no need to hide. Just be considerate.
50. **Commute smart.** If you drive at iftar time, know your countdown so you can pull over safely to break the fast if needed.
