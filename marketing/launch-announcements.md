### Product Hunt comment (first comment / maker message)

Thanks for checking out TryRamadan! 🌙

We built it because we wanted one place for the whole Ramadan experience: prayer times, fasting countdown, suhoor & iftar planning, and cultural learning — for both Muslims and non-Muslims who want to try fasting.

What you can do:
• Set your location → get accurate prayer times and suhoor/iftar cutoffs
• Track your fast with a live countdown and "I'm fasting" / "Break fast" flow
• Explore recipes and traditions from different countries
• Follow a Quran reading plan (juz per day) with links to Quran.com
• Export events to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook

It's free, works in the browser (PWA), and we'd love your feedback. Ramadan Mubarak!
