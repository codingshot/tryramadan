# Seasonal marketing calendar — TryRamadan

**Internal use only.** When to run what, by phase. Ramadan dates shift ~11 days earlier each Gregorian year; adjust annually.

---

## Timeline (approximate)

| Phase | Timing (Gregorian) | Focus |
|-------|--------------------|--------|
| **Pre-Ramadan** | 4–6 weeks before Ramadan starts | Awareness, “get ready,” first-time tryers |
| **Ramadan week –1** | Last 7 days before day 1 | Countdown, installs, reminders |
| **Ramadan day 1** | First day of fasting | Launch / “Ramadan Mubarak,” peak acquisition |
| **Ramadan week 1** | Days 1–7 | Onboarding, feature tips, “how’s it going?” |
| **Mid-Ramadan** | ~Day 14–15 | Re-engage, halfway message, streak/progress |
| **Last 10 nights** | Days 21–29/30 | Laylat al-Qadr, reflective tone, final push |
| **Eid** | First day after Ramadan | Eid Mubarak, thank you, reviews, “see you next year” |
| **Off-season** | Rest of year | Evergreen content, SEO, build list, plan next year |

---

## Pre-Ramadan (4–6 weeks out)

**Goals:** Build awareness; get “try Ramadan” and “Ramadan app” in front of people; grow list or followers.

**Content ideas:**
- “Ramadan 20XX is in X weeks. Here’s how to prepare.”
- Feature spotlights: prayer times, countdown, meal planning, Quran (one per week).
- “First time fasting? What to know before Ramadan.”
- Glossary teasers: Suhoor, Iftar, Ramadan Mubarak.
- Any new feature or app launch: “TryRamadan is ready for Ramadan 20XX.”

**Channels:** Blog/SEO, Instagram grid + Stories, Twitter, Facebook. Optional: light paid (interest targeting “Ramadan,” “Islamic,” “intermittent fasting”).

**CTAs:** Download / open app; “Set your location and reminders before day 1.”

---

## Ramadan week –1 (countdown week)

**Goals:** Maximize installs and onboarding before fasting starts.

**Content ideas:**
- Daily or every-other-day countdown: “7 days,” “5 days,” “Tomorrow,” “Ramadan starts tonight.”
- “Last chance to set your location and suhoor/iftar reminders.”
- Short video or carousel: “What to do in TryRamadan before day 1.”

**Channels:** All social; push or email if available.

**CTAs:** “Get the app now,” “Set your location,” “Turn on reminders.”

---

## Ramadan day 1

**Goals:** Big “Ramadan Mubarak” moment; drive installs and day-1 opens.

**Content ideas:**
- “Ramadan Mubarak. Start your journey with TryRamadan.”
- One-liner + link (see social-announcements.md).
- Optional: short video or graphic with countdown + prayer times + “For everyone.”

**Channels:** All social; consider paid boost for this day only.

**CTAs:** “Open the app,” “Start tracking your fast,” “Get prayer times.”

---

## Ramadan weeks 1–2

**Goals:** Activation and habit-building; show value (countdown, prayer times, meals).

**Content ideas:**
- “How’s your first week? Here’s how to get the most from TryRamadan.”
- One tip per post: break-fast reminder, prayer times, meal plan, journal.
- Light engagement: “Suhoor or iftar — which do you plan first?”
- Recipe or culture post: “Iftar inspiration from [region].”

**Channels:** Instagram, Twitter, Facebook. Optional: in-app or email tip.

**CTAs:** “Open the app,” “Log your fast,” “Explore recipes.”

---

## Mid-Ramadan (~day 14–15)

**Goals:** Re-engage lapsing users; celebrate progress.

**Content ideas:**
- “We’re halfway. You’re doing great.”
- “Check your progress in the app — streak, days completed, hours fasted.”
- “Still need prayer times or countdown? We’re here.”

**Channels:** All social; optional email or in-app message.

**CTAs:** “Open TryRamadan,” “See your progress.”

---

## Last 10 nights (days 21–29/30)

**Goals:** Reflective, supportive tone; Laylat al-Qadr awareness; no hard sell.

**Content ideas:**
- “The last 10 nights are here. We’re still here with your prayer times and countdown.”
- “Laylat al-Qadr — may these nights be blessed.”
- Gentle reminder of app value (prayer times, consistency).

**Channels:** All social. Keep copy short and respectful.

**CTAs:** “Open the app,” “Share with someone fasting.”

---

## Eid

**Goals:** Thank the community; close the loop; ask for reviews; tease next year.

**Content ideas:**
- “Eid Mubarak from everyone at TryRamadan.”
- “Thank you for fasting with us this Ramadan.”
- “See you next year — we’ll be here.”
- Optional: “What should we add for next Ramadan?” (survey or reply prompt).
- “If TryRamadan helped you, leave us a review.”

**Channels:** All social; optional email.

**CTAs:** “Leave a review,” “Follow for next Ramadan,” “Share with a friend.”

---

## Off-season (post-Eid until next pre-Ramadan)

**Goals:** SEO, evergreen content, build list, plan next campaign.

**Content ideas:**
- Evergreen blog: “How to try Ramadan fasting,” “What is suhoor and iftar,” “Ramadan fasting and health.”
- Glossary and culture pages for search.
- Rare product post: “TryRamadan — get ready for Ramadan 20XX” (2–3 months before).
- Build email list or partnership list for next year.

**Channels:** Blog, SEO, occasional social post. Low frequency.

**CTAs:** “Save the app for next Ramadan,” “Sign up for a reminder.”
